Kyoto Dining Compass Support

Thank you for using Kyoto Dining Compass.

Kyoto Dining Compass is an independent dining guide app designed to help travelers discover restaurants in Kyoto.

Contact

If you have any questions, feedback, bug reports, or requests regarding Kyoto Dining Compass, please contact us by email.

Email: diningtable.project@gmail.com

Common Support Topics

  • Questions about how to use the app
  • Bug reports or display issues
  • Restaurant information updates
  • Map or reservation link issues
  • Feedback about app features

Restaurant Information

Restaurant information, prices, opening hours, holidays, menu items, and reservation availability may change without notice. Please check the restaurant's official website or reservation page before visiting.

Images

Some images shown in the app may be illustrative images and may not represent the actual restaurant, dishes, or interior.

Independence

Kyoto Dining Compass is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the restaurants listed in the app.
