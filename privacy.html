Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 17, 2026

Kyoto Dining Compass ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App may access your device's location only when you grant permission. Your location is used solely to display nearby restaurants and attractions and to provide directions.

The App does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

How Information Is Used

Location information is used only to provide location-based features within the App. Location data is not stored on our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App may open external services such as Apple Maps, Google Maps, restaurant websites, or reservation websites. These services have their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
